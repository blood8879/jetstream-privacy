SeolaSoft ("we", "us") operates the mobile game Jetstream: Airline Tycoon (package com.seolasoft.jetstream, the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game processes and why, how long we keep it, and how you can control it.

Summary

The Game has no sign-up and does not collect your name, email address, phone number, postal address, contacts, photos, or precise location. We process only an anonymous identifier and your game save so progress can be backed up and transferred, plus analytics and advertising identifiers used to improve the Game and serve ads. You can delete your cloud data inside the Game (Settings → Device Transfer & Cloud Backup → Delete Cloud Data).

2. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information for money and do not provide it to third parties other than the service providers below, who process it on our behalf to run the Game.

3. Service providers

  • Google LLC — Firebase (Anonymous Auth, Cloud Firestore, Analytics, Crashlytics, Remote Config): account-free save backup, usage statistics, crash reporting, remote configuration. Firebase privacy
  • Google LLC — AdMob and User Messaging Platform: rewarded and interstitial ads, ad measurement, consent management. How Google uses data from partner apps

4. International transfers

Game saves are stored in Google Cloud's Seoul region (asia-northeast3, Republic of Korea). Authentication, analytics, and advertising data may be processed in the United States and other countries where Google operates, transmitted over encrypted (TLS) connections whenever you use the Game. Transfers out of the EEA, UK, and Switzerland rely on the EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework adopted by Google.

5. Advertising and your choices

The Game is free and supported by ads: rewarded ads you choose to watch, and interstitial ads shown at natural breaks such as quarterly reports. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, at any time in device Settings → Google → Ads. In regions where it applies, you can also change your choices in the Game under Settings → Ad Privacy Options. Ads still appear after opting out but are not personalized. The Game itself does not use cookies.

6. Deletion

  • In the Game (immediate) — Settings → Device Transfer & Cloud Backup → Delete Cloud Data deletes your server-side save, any unused transfer code, and your anonymous account. Progress on the device remains; reopening the app starts a fresh backup under a new anonymous account.
  • On your device — uninstalling the app removes data stored on the device.
  • By email — send your in-game Support ID to the address below; we will respond within 10 days and complete deletion within 30 days.

Deleted data is erased from the database and cannot be recovered. See Data deletion for step-by-step instructions.

7. Security

  • All traffic is encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS).
  • Server security rules let each anonymous account read and write only its own save.
  • Transfer codes are 10-character, single-use codes generated with a cryptographic random number generator and stop working after 7 days.
  • Administrative access is limited to the operator, and we process only the minimum data needed.

8. Children

The Game is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (under 14 in Korea, or the applicable age in your country). If we learn that we have, we will delete it promptly.

9. EEA, UK, and Switzerland

SeolaSoft is the controller. Our legal bases are: performance of a contract for cloud backup and device transfer you request; consent for analytics storage, ad storage, and personalized ads (collected through the consent message; until you answer, analytics and ad storage start as denied under Google Consent Mode); and our legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention, and serving non-personalized ads. You have the right to access, rectify, erase, restrict, port, and object to processing, to withdraw consent at any time (Settings → Ad Privacy Options), and to lodge a complaint with your local supervisory authority.
